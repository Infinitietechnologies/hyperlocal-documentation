📱 Introduction — Seller App (Flutter)

The Seller App is a powerful mobile application built with Flutter, enabling vendors to efficiently manage their store operations, products, and orders directly from their mobile devices.

Overview

The Seller App acts as the central hub for vendors to manage their business within the hyperlocal platform. Built with Flutter, it ensures:

Native performance on both iOS and Android
Consistent and intuitive user experience
Fast and responsive interface for quick operations
Real-time order updates and notifications
Efficient store and inventory management on the go

It allows sellers to focus on managing their products and fulfilling orders, while the platform handles delivery and logistics.

Key Features
1. Store Management
Store profile setup and management
Business hours configuration
Store availability toggle (open/close)
Store information and media management

2. Product Management
Add, edit, and delete products
Category and subcategory assignment
Product variants and pricing management
Stock and inventory control

3. Order Management
Real-time order notifications
Accept or reject incoming orders
Order status updates (e.g., accept, reject, prepare)
Order history and detailed view
Order preparation tracking

4. Subscription Management
View available subscription plans
Purchase and activate subscription plans
Track subscription status and validity
Access features based on active plan

4.Earnings & Reports
Earnings overview
Sales insights and summaries
Transaction history
Commission tracking
