# Seller Panel Overview

This guide provides a walkthrough of the Seller Panel features, helping you manage your store, products, orders, and financials effectively.

## 1. Dashboard

The Dashboard offers a comprehensive visual overview of your business performance.

- **Business Insights**: View graphs displaying store revenue and store-wise order distribution.
- **Recent Orders**: Quickly access and review your most recent orders.

![Dashboard Overview](/images/seller-overview/dashboard.png)

## 2. Wallet

The Wallet section displays your current wallet balance available for withdrawal or platform usage.

![Wallet Balance](/images/seller-overview/wallet.png)

## 3. Withdrawals

Manage your fund withdrawals in this section.

- **Make a Withdrawal**: Request a payout from your wallet balance.
- **Withdrawal History**: View a log of all past withdrawal requests and their statuses.

![Withdrawals](/images/seller-overview/withdrawals.png)

## 4. Settlements

Track your earnings and deductions.

- **Earnings & Deductions**: View detailed breakdowns of your settlements.
- **Settlement History**: Access a history of all processed settlements.

![Settlements](/images/seller-overview/settlements.png)

## 5. Orders

Manage orders placed for your store.

- **View Orders**: See a list of all orders assigned to your logged-in store account.

![Orders List](/images/seller-overview/orders.png)

## 6. Return Orders

Manage customer return requests.

- **Return Requests**: View and process details for any order return requests.

![Return Orders](/images/seller-overview/return-orders.png)

## 7. Categories

View the list of product categories available for your listings. This helps you understand where your products fits in the marketplace structure.

![Categories List](/images/seller-overview/categories.png)

## 8. Brands

View the list of available brands you can associate with your products.

![Brands List](/images/seller-overview/brands.png)

## 9. Attributes

Manage product attributes (e.g., size, color, material) to create detailed product variations.

- **Attribute List**: View existing attributes and attribute types.
- **Add Attribute**: Create new attributes and attribute types to customize your product specification.

![Attributes](/images/seller-overview/attributes.png)

## 10. Manage Products

This section allows you to complete product management.

### Add New Product

To create a product, click **Add Product** and follow the multi-step wizard:

1.  **Select Category**: Choose the appropriate category for your product.
2.  **Product Info**: Enter details like **Title**, **Brand**, and other custom fields.
3.  **Policies and Features**: Define return policies and key product features.
4.  **Variations**: Add product variants (e.g., Small, Medium, Large) if applicable.
5.  **Images**: Upload high-quality product images.
6.  **Description**: Add detailed **Short** and **Long Descriptions**.
7.  **Pricing & Tags**: Set the base pricing and tax information.
8.  **Store Pricing**: You must set the Price, Special Price, Cost, Stock, and SKU for **each store** you manage.

![Add Product Wizard](/images/seller-overview/add-product-wizard.png)
![Store Pricing](/images/seller-overview/store-pricing.png)

## 11. Product FAQ

Create and manage Frequently Asked Questions for your specific products to help customers make informed decisions.

- **Create FAQ**: Add new questions and answers related to your products.

![Product FAQ](/images/seller-overview/product-faq.png)

## 12. Tax Rates

View the list of applicable tax rates. This helps you understand the tax structures applied to your products.

![Tax Rates](/images/seller-overview/tax-rates.png)

## 13. Store Management

Manage your physical or digital store details.

- **Create Store**: Click **Add New Store**, fill in the necessary details (e.g., Location, Contact Info), and create it.
- **Store List**: View and edit the details of your created stores.

![Store Management](/images/seller-overview/store-management.png)

## 14. Roles & Permissions

Manage access control for your store staff.

- **Roles**: View the permissions associated with your seller role.
- **Manage Roles**: If you need to delegate, you can create new roles (e.g., Store Manager) and assign specific permissions.
- **System Users**: Create new user accounts for your staff, assigning them a **Name**, **Email**, **Mobile**, **Password**, and a specific **Role**.

![Roles and Permissions](/images/seller-overview/roles-permissions.png)
