# Admin Panel Overview

This guide provides a walkthrough of the essential features in the Admin Panel, including configuring system settings, managing delivery zones, creating categories and brands, and setting up stores.

## 1. Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a comprehensive visual overview of your platform's performance.

- **Business Insights**: View graphs and bar charts representing overall sales, earnings, and order statistics.
- **Top Performers**: Quickly access lists of:
  - Top Rated Sellers
  - Top Selling Products
  - Top Categories

![Dashboard Overview](/images/admin-overview/dashboard.png)

## 2. General System Settings

The System Settings section allows you to configure core aspects of your application, such as the app name, timezone, currency, and branding (logos).

1.  Navigate to **Settings** -> **System** in the sidebar.
2.  Fill in the required fields:
    - **App Name**: The name of your platform.
    - **System Timezone**: Your operating time zone.
    - **Currency**: The currency used for transactions.
    - **Logo & Favicon**: Upload your platform's branding assets.
3.  Click **Save** to apply changes.

You should also explore other settings under the **Settings** menu, such as **Authentication**, **Email**, and **Payment** configurations, to ensure your system is fully operational.

![System Settings](/images/admin-overview/system-settings.png)

## 3. Adding Delivery Zones

Delivery zones define the geographic areas where your service operates.

1.  Log in to your **Admin Panel**.
2.  Navigate to **Delivery Zones** in the sidebar.
3.  Click on **+ Add Delivery Zone**.
4.  Fill in the necessary details (e.g., Zone Name, Coordinates/Map selection).
5.  **Save** the zone.

![Delivery Zones List](/images/admin-overview/delivery-zones.png)

## 4. Creating Categories

Categories help organize products for easier browsing.

1.  Navigate to the **Categories** section.
2.  Click **Add Category**.
3.  Enter the **Name** of the category.
4.  Select whether this is a **Parent Category** or a **Child Category** (Sub-category).
5.  **Product Verification**: Enable this option if you want to require admin approval for products added to this category.
6.  Click **Save**.

![Categories](/images/admin-overview/categories.png)

## 5. Creating Brands

Brands allow you to associate products with specific manufacturers or labels.

1.  Navigate to the **Brands** section.
2.  Click **Add Brand**.
3.  Fill in the brand details (Name, Logo).
4.  **Scope**: Choose the visibility scope:
    - **Global**: The brand is available across all categories.
    - **Category Specific**: The brand is only available for specific categories.
5.  Click **Save**.

![Brands](/images/admin-overview/brands.png)

## 6. Seller Management

Manage your sellers, view their earnings, and process withdrawals.

### Manage Sellers

To create a new seller, click on **Add Seller** and fill in basic details like name, location, and contact info. All existing sellers can be viewed in the **Sellers** table.

![Sellers List](/images/admin-overview/sellers-lists.png)

### Settlements (Earnings & Deductions)

You can check seller earnings and deductions in the **Settlement** overview. This allows you to view payouts and returns.

![Payouts](/images/admin-overview/payment-settlement.png)

### Seller Withdrawals

In the **Seller Withdrawal** section, you can see all pending withdrawal requests. You can view the details of a request and process it. A history of all withdrawals is available in the **Withdrawal History** tab.

![Pending Withdrawal Requests](/images/admin-overview/pending-withdrawals.png)

## 7. Stores

All stores can be viewed in the **Stores** section. The Admin has the ability to **Approve** or **Reject** a store application.

![Stores List](/images/admin-overview/stores.png)

## 8. Products

This section lists all products from all sellers.

- If **Product Verification** is enabled for a category, products will appear with a "Pending Verification" status.
- The Admin can review these products and choose to **Approve** or **Reject** them.

![Products List](/images/admin-overview/products.png)

## 9. Orders

The Orders section allows you to track and manage all customer orders placed on the platform.

- **View Orders**: See a comprehensive list of all placed orders.
- **Order Details**: Click on a specific order to view detailed information.
- **Invoice**: You can generate and check the invoice for any order directly from this section.

![Orders List](/images/admin-overview/orders.png)

## 10. Tax Rates

Manage tax configurations for your platform.

- **Normal Tax**: Click **Create Tax** and fill in the title and percentage.
- **Group Tax**: Click **Create Group Tax**, enter a title, and select multiple sub-taxes to combine them into a single group tax.

![Tax Rates](/images/admin-overview/tax-rates.png)

## 11. Manage Delivery Boys

Manage your delivery fleet, their earnings, and cash collections.

### Delivery Boy List

View the list of all delivery boys in the **Delivery Boy** section.

### Earnings & Payments

In the **Delivery Boy Earning** section, you can view earnings and process payments. A history of all earnings is available in **Earning History**.

### Cash Collection

View **Pending Cash Collections** to see cash collected by delivery boys (e.g., from COD orders). You can process the submission of this cash. Once submitted, it appears in the **Cash Collection History**.

![Pending Cash Collections](/images/admin-overview/pending-cash-collections.png)

### Cash Withdrawals

In **Delivery Boy Cash Withdrawals**, you can view and process withdrawal requests. Past transactions are logged in **Withdrawal History**.

## 12. Managing Banners

Banners are used to display promotional images on the home screen or specific sections.

1.  Navigate to the **Banners** section.
2.  Click **Add Banner**.
3.  Fill in the necessary details (Image, Link/Action, Position).
4.  Click **Save**.

![Banners](/images/admin-overview/banners.png)

## 13. Managing Promos

Promos (Promocodes) allow you to offer discounts to users at the cart page.

1.  Navigate to the **Promos** section.
2.  Click **Add New Promo**.
3.  Enter details like Code, Discount, Usage Limit, and Validity.
4.  **Save** the promo code.

![Promos](/images/admin-overview/promos.png)

## 14. Featured Sections

Featured sections allow you to highlight specific products or categories on the home page.

1.  Navigate to the **Featured Sections** menu.
2.  Click **Add Featured Section**.
3.  Fill in details like Title, Type (Global/Category-wise), and Products.
4.  Click **Save**.

![Featured Sections](/images/admin-overview/featured-sections.png)

## 15. FAQ

Manage Frequently Asked Questions for your platform.

1.  Navigate to the **FAQ** section.
2.  Click **Add FAQ**.
3.  Fill in the question and answer details.
4.  Click **Create**.

![FAQ](/images/admin-overview/faq.png)

## 16. Roles & Permissions

Manage access control for the Admin Panel.

- **Roles**: View existing roles (e.g., Admin) and their permissions.
  - To create a new role (e.g., Manager), click **Create Role**, give it a name, and assign specific permissions.
- **System Users**: Create new system users with Name, Email, Mobile, Password, and assign them a **Role**.

![Roles and Permissions](/images/admin-overview/roles-permissions.png)

## 17. System Updates

Keep your Admin Panel up to date.

1.  Navigate to **System Updates**.
2.  View the **Current Version** of your system.
3.  Upload the update file to apply the latest patches and features.

![System Updates](/images/admin-overview/system-updates.png)

Click **“Exit”** to access the admin panel and start managing your hyperlocal marketplace.
